The Metro Mareng EMS Caravan 2026 is Metro Supermarket's premier enterprise convention crafted specifically for sari-sari store owners, wholesalers, resellers, and institutional suki partners.

On October 2, 2026, entry is exclusively reserved for active Mareng EMS Members and Metro Business Member card holders. On October 3, 2026, entry is OPEN SA TANAN (open to all business owners, sari-sari store operators, and general shoppers).
No online pre-registration is needed! Registration will take place strictly on-site at the venue gates (The Tent at Mandani Bay) starting October 2, 2026 at 8:00 AM.
You can register or apply for membership directly at any Metro Supermarket customer service counter, or sign up at our designated membership booths during the Caravan event!
The event runs from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on both days (October 2 and October 3, 2026) at The Tent at Mandani Bay.
